The format

Every trainer slot was driven by its own agent process, seeing only the engine's fog-of-war projection. Neither side could read the other's roster; a third agent refereed each match and audited the engine while it ran.

The bracket

Three matches put all six teams on the board at once. The three survivors were reseeded: the most dominant Round 1 winner took the bye, the other two fought for the right to meet it.

The ladder

Ranked by the round each team reached, then — since everyone below third left with nothing standing — by how much of the winner's roster it took down on the way out.

The organiser's desk

Colophon

Every battle here was resolved by PokéArena's own engine — a pure (state, actionP1, actionP2) → (newState, events) function, so each match replays bit-for-bit from its seed. The tournament ran through cmd/royale, a file-backed broker: one match directory, one lock file, and two commands per player. view renders nothing but the engine's fog-of-war projection, so a contestant physically could not see the opposing bench; the referee commands sat behind a per-match judge token.
