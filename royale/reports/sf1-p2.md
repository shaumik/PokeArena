## Verdict
Won on turn 15 with Cloyster untouched at 157/157. Meridian's whole roster fell: Exeggutor, Rapidash, Vileplume, Flareon, Moltres, Ninetales. A mono-fire Drought team.

## Game plan
Two Rooms, spent hard, plus one rule my predecessor learned the expensive way: never pay for a switch. Every entry I made except one was free — after a faint, when the opponent cannot punish it. Mr. Mime was expendable and got exposed; Porygon was the last setter and got banked behind a full-HP body the moment Room One had done its work.

## What actually happened
Ninetales led, set sun, and Fire Blast put Mr. Mime in kill range immediately, so I cashed the Mime in for chip and a free replacement instead of pointlessly protecting it. Marowak did the heavy lifting: Rock Head makes Double-Edge recoil-free, so it beat Earthquake against a team that kept pivoting to airborne threats. Their own sun then powered my Fire Punch through Exeggutor.

The turn that decided the match was turn 6. Room One had one tick left and Rapidash was in; I switched Porygon out at full HP rather than let it grind down. That single conserved setter became Room Two, and Room Two was the whole endgame — Porygon chipped Ninetales, traded itself for a free Cloyster entry, and Cloyster closed with priority.

## The mistake
Turn 10. I switched Omastar into Ninetales reasoning that Rock/Water eats fire at 0.25x. It does — but Solar Beam is instant in sun and 4x on Omastar. It died on entry without acting, and I had already seen Solar Beam from two other Pokémon on that roster. Against Drought, a 4x grass weakness is not a wall, it is a coin flip, and I should have sent Porygon there — it takes both fire and grass neutrally and I proved that one turn later.

## Note to my successor
Free entries only. A switch made after a faint costs zero; a switch made into a live attacker costs a Pokémon. Second: against sun, treat every fire type as if it holds Solar Beam until proven otherwise — resistances lie when the weather rewrites a charge move. Third: Marowak's Double-Edge, not Earthquake, is the default; Rock Head makes it free and nothing is immune to it. Last: the Room's final tick is for damage you can guarantee, never for a switch.
