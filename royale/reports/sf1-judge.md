# Semifinal sf1 — Meridian (sun) vs The Low Ceiling (trick room)

Seed 5711 · turn cap 60 · ended turn 15 · referee token `judge-sf1-5711`

## Verdict

**The Low Ceiling wins, 1–0.** Cloyster is the last thing standing on either
side, and it finishes the match at 100% HP without ever having been hit. Every
one of Meridian's six is on the floor by turn 15.

The scoreline flatters nobody. Meridian traded evenly for eight turns and then
lost the endgame in two decisions: it spent Rapidash's body to bring Machamp to
3 HP that Machamp's own Flame Orb was going to take anyway, and it walked a 25%
Moltres into a Porygon that had spent two straight turns declining to attack.
The sun did everything the theme promised — it just ran out four turns before
the Trick Room did.

## The story

**Turns 1–2 — the button and the finger on it.** Ninetales opens by setting
eight turns of noon and immediately firing at Mr. Mime, which is exactly the
right target and exactly one turn too slow: Trick Room is priority −7, so it
resolves after the Fire Blast no matter what, and Mime eats 94 and presses the
button anyway. Turn 2 Mime, now the *fastest* thing on the field by virtue of
being the slowest, gets a free Psychic in for 78 before Ninetales finishes it.
Meridian trades its lead's health for the setter and calls it even. It is not
even. There is a second setter.

**Turns 3–5 — Marowak in the room.** Thick Club doubles a Brave 252 Atk
Marowak to 290, and inside Trick Room it moves first every turn. Exeggutor
comes in to wall Ground and instead takes 135 from a recoil-free Double-Edge
(Rock Head, no chip on Marowak at all), burns its Sitrus, and dies the next
turn to a *Fire Punch* — Meridian's own sun powering the move that kills its
Grass type. That's the cruellest line in the match. Moltres answers with an
instant, sun-charged Solar Beam for a clean 4x-adjacent kill on the bone, but
eats a Double-Edge on the way in and is left at 25% — a number that decides
the game ten turns later.

**Turns 6–8 — the burn that killed its own owner.** Porygon comes in, Traces
Flame Body off Moltres, and relights Trick Room. Rapidash is sent to break it
and instead meets a Machamp switch-in: Charcoal + noon + STAB Flare Blitz for
194 leaves Machamp on 3 HP, and Rapidash takes 64 recoil. Then Flame Orb
burns Machamp — turning on Guts — and turn 8 a Guts-boosted Close Combat
erases Rapidash outright before the burn residual takes Machamp's last 3 HP
in the same breath. Two bodies, one turn, both from the same item. The sun
faded at the end of that turn: exactly eight turns, exactly as advertised.

**Turns 9–10 — Cloyster eats the middle.** With the sun gone Vileplume's
Chlorophyll is worth nothing, and Vileplume is a 2x Ice target with a
Skill Link Icicle Spear pointed at it. Four hits (50/44/54/3) and it's over
before the fifth is needed. Flareon comes in to Superpower the shell and never
gets to move: a STAB Liquidation at 2x removes it in one. Cloyster is 2-for-2
without taking damage, and the second Trick Room expires having cost Meridian
two Pokémon for nothing.

**Turns 11–13 — the Solar Beam and the misread.** Ninetales returns, Drought
relights the sky, and an instant Solar Beam at 4x deletes a freshly-switched
Omastar before Weakness Policy can even become relevant. That's the high point.
Then Meridian, reading a Porygon that had used Trick Room twice and attacked
zero times, sent Moltres in at 25% to Roost behind it. Porygon attacked. Tri
Attack for 63-plus against a 41 HP Moltres. That switch is where the match
ended.

**Turns 14–15 — priority closes it.** Ninetales, alone, trades Fire Blast for
Tri Attack and takes a 20% paralysis on the way, dropping to 8 HP and half
Speed inside a Trick Room where being slow was already the only currency. It
kills Porygon on the way down. Cloyster comes in and, rather than gamble on a
90% Rock Blast, throws Ice Shard — priority +1, which wins its bracket whether
the dimensions are twisted or not. 8 damage, not very effective, dead anyway.

## MVP

**Cloyster.** Three KOs, zero damage taken, two separate entries and it left
the field at full HP both times. It never needed the King's Rock flinches it
was built for and it never needed the fifth Icicle Spear hit either. The
closing Ice Shard is the tell: with the match already won it still picked the
100%-accuracy option over the bigger number. Honourable mention to **Machamp**,
which achieved a 1-for-1 trade and then died to its own held item in the same
turn, and to **Ninetales**, which scored three of Meridian's four KOs and was
the only thing on that team that ever looked like winning.

## Notable turns

- **Turn 1** — Fire Blast connects for 94 and Trick Room goes up anyway.
  Priority −7 means the setter cannot be outsped, only outlasted, and Meridian
  never had a turn where that wasn't true.
- **Turn 4** — *Fire Punch*, not Earthquake, kills Exeggutor. Marowak borrowed
  Meridian's own ×1.5 sun boost to remove Meridian's Grass type.
- **Turn 5** — Solar Beam fires the turn it's chosen ("Moltres took in
  sunlight!" immediately followed by the attack) and 4x's Marowak off the
  board for 167 exact. The first Trick Room expires on the same turn.
- **Turn 8** — Machamp's Flame Orb both wins it a KO (Guts-boosted Close
  Combat) and kills it (burn residual, −3 off 3 HP) inside a single turn, and
  the sun runs out at the end of it. Three timers hit zero at once.
- **Turn 11** — Omastar switches in and is deleted by a 4x instant Solar Beam
  for exactly its 177 max HP. Weakness Policy never gets to fire, correctly —
  the holder has to survive the hit.
- **Turn 13** — the switch that lost the match. Moltres at 41 HP goes in
  behind a Porygon that had not attacked once, and Porygon attacks.
- **Turn 15** — Ice Shard. Priority beats Trick Room's inversion because
  inversion only reorders *within* a bracket. 8 damage, resisted, lethal.

## BUGS

**CONFIRMED — Trace's copied ability is permanent; it never reverts on
switch-out, so Trace fires at most once per battle.**

Observed: at resolution #8 (turn 5 replace) Porygon switched in and logged
"Porygon's Trace copied Moltres's Flame Body!". Porygon then switched *out* on
turn 7 and switched back in at resolution #18 (turn 11 replace) directly
opposite a Drought Ninetales. No Trace line was printed and no ability was
copied. Canon (and this engine's own comment) is that Trace re-triggers on
every switch-in; the copy is not permanent.

Proof in source. `abilities.go:385` overwrites the tracer's own ability in
place:

```go
p.Ability = foe.Ability
```

and there is no stored original. A repo-wide search for assignments to
`.Ability` returns exactly two sites — `battle.go:594` (team construction) and
`abilities.go:385` (Trace) — so nothing ever writes it back. The switch-out
path `doSwitchWithCarry` (`switching.go:35`–`95`) resets `Stages`, `Volatiles`
and the Toxic counter, and deliberately preserves the sleep counter, but never
touches `Ability`. Dispatch then reads the mutated field: `applyOnSwitchIn`
(`abilities.go:1465`–`1473`) calls `abilityOf(user)`, so on re-entry Porygon
ran Flame Body's (nonexistent) switch-in hook instead of Trace's.

Effect in this match: Porygon spent turns 11–14 illegally holding Flame Body —
a 30% contact-burn rider it should not have had — instead of Trace. It is
mitigated here only by luck: the ability it would have copied was Drought,
which is a no-op against already-active sun (see below), and Meridian threw no
contact moves at Porygon in that window. The defect is not mitigated in
general — a Trace user that pivots is permanently locked to its first copy, and
against Intimidate or a weather setter on a later switch-in that is a live
competitive difference.

**NOT-A-BUG — Harvest never returned Exeggutor's Sitrus Berry.**

Observed: Exeggutor ate its Sitrus on turn 3 under harsh sun and no Harvest
proc appeared at that turn's residual, where canon gives a 100% restore in sun.
This is a declared, documented engine limitation rather than a defect:
`abilities.go:287` registers `"harvest": {Kind: "harvest"}` with no hooks, and
the block above it (`abilities.go:265`–`287`) explicitly lists Harvest under
"recognized but inert", blocked on berry *manipulation* infrastructure that
does not exist yet. Flagging it only because Meridian's team blurb sells
Harvest as a pillar of the strategy — team builders should know it is currently
a null ability, but the engine is behaving as written.

**NOT-A-BUG — Drought printed nothing when Ninetales re-entered at
resolution #21 with sun already up.**

`setWeatherFromAbility` (`abilities.go:1331`–`1334`) returns immediately when
`s.Weather.Kind` already equals the incoming kind, emitting no log and, crucially,
*not* refreshing the timer. That matches Showdown, where `field.setWeather`
fails on a same-weather set. Confirmed against the counters in the report dump:
sun read `sun(5)` before and after that switch-in.

**NOT-A-BUG — the "It's super effective!" line prints after the damage line,
where Showdown emits `|-supereffective|` before `|-damage|`.**

Cosmetic log ordering only; every effectiveness multiplier was numerically
correct in the damage checks below. Noting it so a future referee doesn't
re-derive it.

### Checked and cleared

Everything below was verified against the formula, the source, or both, and is
correct.

- **Trick Room vs speed, the core matchup interaction.** `goesFirst`
  (`turn.go:337`–`367`) resolves the priority bracket *first*, then computes
  `effectiveSpeed(...) * sideSpeedMult(...)` for both sides, and only then
  applies `if trickRoomActive(s) { return sx < sy }`. The inversion is on the
  fully-modified stat, not the base. `effectiveSpeed` (`damage.go:83`–`98`)
  layers stages → paralysis ×0.5 → `abilitySpeedMult` (Chlorophyll's sun ×2)
  → `itemSpeedMult` before returning, so a Chlorophyll sweeper in sun under
  Trick Room is correctly made *worse* by its own ability. Speed ties still
  break by RNG under Trick Room, which is canon.
- **Priority still wins its bracket under Trick Room.** Turn 15 Ice Shard (+1)
  moved before Ninetales (0) with Trick Room up. Correct per the priority check
  preceding the inversion.
- **Trick Room duration — exactly five ticks counting the setting turn, twice.**
  TR #1 set turn 1, ended at the residual of turn 5. TR #2 set turn 6, ended at
  the residual of turn 10. `defaultPseudoWeatherTurns` decremented at
  `pseudoweather.go:161`–`165`.
- **Second Trick Room ends rather than extends.** Not exercised in play (the
  room was always down when re-set), but `applyTrickRoomSetter`
  (`pseudoweather.go:89`–`97`) nils the timer and prints "The twisted dimensions
  returned to normal!" on a re-set, with no "But it failed".
- **Heat Rock — exactly eight turns, twice.** Counters from the report dump:
  first sun ran `sun(7)` after turn 1 down to `sun(1)` after turn 7 and cleared
  at the residual of turn 8; second sun was set in the turn-10 replace phase at
  `sun(8)` and was still `sun(3)` when the match ended on turn 15.
- **Solar Beam skips its charge only in sun.** Turns 5 and 11 both logged
  "took in sunlight!" immediately followed by the attack in the same turn, and
  Solar Beam's *power* was correctly left unboosted by sun (the ×1.5 is
  Fire-only): 167 on Marowak and 177 on Omastar both land in the unboosted
  ranges.
- **Fire ×1.5 in sun.** Turn 1 Fire Blast 94 (window 88–105), turn 12 Fire
  Blast 130 (window 130–155, a minimum roll), turn 7 Flare Blitz 194 (window
  186–220 with Charcoal ×1.2 folded into base power before the weather mod).
  All consistent with pokeRound at each modifier boundary.
- **Thick Club.** Marowak's 145 Atk doubled to 290 reproduces Double-Edge 135
  on Exeggutor (window 124–146) and 125 on Moltres (window 119–141) exactly.
- **Rock Head + recoil off damage actually dealt.** Marowak took zero recoil
  from two Double-Edges. Rapidash's Flare Blitz dealt 194 and took 64 recoil —
  ⌊194/3⌋, computed off damage dealt, not off a theoretical roll.
- **Guts + Flame Orb + burn, no double-count.** `burnHalvesAttack`
  (`damage.go:442`–`444`) is the single predicate consulted by both the damage
  path (`damage.go:428`) and Guts' own multiplier (`abilities.go:1261`–`1270`),
  which multiplies by 2.0 to cancel the halve and 1.5 for the boost only when
  the halve actually applied. Facade is excluded from the halve by the same
  predicate (`m.ID != "facade"`), so a burned Guts Facade doubles off the status
  without eating the cut and without being un-cut twice. Facade was never
  thrown in this match; the interaction is clean by construction.
- **Skill Link + King's Rock.** Icicle Spear on turn 9 stopped at four hits
  solely because Vileplume fainted on the fourth ("Hit 4 time(s)!"); Skill Link
  forces the maximum via `abilityMaxesMultihit` (`abilities.go:1204`,
  `turn.go:974`–`978`). The King's Rock roll is inside the per-strike apply
  path — `applyItemOnDealtDamage` at `turn.go:1406` sits in the single-hit
  routine called once per strike, so the 10% is rolled per hit, not per move.
  Per-hit damage variance was also independent (50/44/54, window 44–54).
- **Weakness Policy correctly did not fire** when a 4x Solar Beam killed
  Omastar outright on turn 11 — the holder must survive the hit.
- **Sitrus Berry.** Exeggutor +50 off 201 max and Porygon +43 off 172 max are
  both ⌊MaxHP/4⌋, each firing at the ≤50% threshold.
- **Status legality.** Machamp's Flame Orb burn landed on a non-Fire type;
  Ninetales was paralysed by Tri Attack's 20% secondary, which is legal
  (only Electric types resist paralysis). No freeze was rolled, so the
  harsh-sun freeze block was not exercised. Burn residual 1/16 of max HP,
  correctly clamped to Machamp's last 3 HP.
- **Speed order, every turn.** Recomputed independently for all 15 turns from
  base stats, natures, 0-Speed IV/EV spreads, the Trick Room windows and
  Ninetales' turn-14 paralysis. Every ordering matches.
- **Damage, every attack.** All eleven connecting attacks reproduce inside the
  85–100 roll window of the Gen-3+ formula with the correct STAB, type,
  weather, item and ability modifiers.
- **Format clauses / hidden information.** No sleep, no evasion, no OHKO
  moves, no duplicate species. Abilities and items were revealed only as they
  fired (Trace announced itself and revealed both sides, per the comment at
  `abilities.go:380`–`386`). No PP violation observed: Ninetales' Fire Blast
  saw 4 of 5 uses, Trick Room 1 + 2 of 5 across two setters.
- **Stuck-match watch: nothing to report.** All 24 resolutions arrived without
  a stall; no `-wait` call timed out and `status` never showed a side owed an
  action for longer than a normal decision.

Not exercised this match, so neither confirmed nor cleared: Flash Fire (no
Fire move was aimed at Flareon or Rapidash), Mental Herb (nothing Taunted or
Encored Mr. Mime), Encore, weather-keyed *residual* abilities on the weather's
final turn (neither roster fields Solar Power, Dry Skin or Ice Body), and
Moltres' Flame Body, which had one legitimate 30% roll against Marowak's
turn-5 Double-Edge and missed it.
