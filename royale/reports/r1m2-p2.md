# r1m2 — The Low Ceiling (p2) vs The Caltrops

## Verdict
Win, turn 20 of 60. Two Pokémon standing to zero — a poisoned Marowak and an untouched Omastar.

## Game plan
Set Trick Room turn one with Mr. Mime — Mental Herb makes a Taunt a wasted turn — then spend the four fast turns swinging. Against a hazard team the real discipline was simpler: The Caltrops win by making you switch, so don't. Leave the setter in until it dies and take free replacements, never paid ones.

## What actually happened
Mr. Mime set the Room while Sandslash laid rocks, then took Sandslash to 30%, cracked Dragonite's Multiscale, hit it twice with Dazzling Gleam and chipped Golem before dying — four Pokémon damaged for the price of one. Porygon came in free, re-set the Room, Traced Sheer Force off Nidoqueen and fired it back as a boosted Ice Beam, killed Weezing, then traded itself to Gengar's Destiny Bond.

After that the Room was gone for good and I was the slowest team on the field — but holding four full-HP bruisers against four cripples. Cloyster's Ice Shard became the substitute Trick Room: priority ice, super effective on three of their four survivors, with a King's Rock flinch stealing a bonus turn. Machamp treated the Toxic Spikes as a gift — Guts Facade is 210 power — and flattened Golem. Marowak closed on Dragonite.

## The mistake
Turn 12 I let Porygon eat Gengar's Taunt and so never got a third Trick Room. A fast ghost on a hazard team is always carrying Taunt, and the Room was on its last tick anyway — I should have pivoted Porygon out and held it in reserve. Instead I fed my last setter to a Destiny Bond and played the back half with no speed control at all. Their team was already broken, so it cost nothing. Against an intact one it loses.

## Note to my successor
Mr. Mime's Mental Herb is one-shot anti-Taunt insurance; Porygon has none. Expose the Mime, protect the Porygon. Never press Trick Room while it is already up — the engine treats the second use as a toggle and cancels it. Five ticks includes the setting turn, so it is four working turns per use. Cloyster is the MVP outside the Room: Ice Shard is your only priority, and King's Rock rolls its flinch on every one of Skill Link's five hits. Machamp genuinely wants to be poisoned. Marowak's Earthquake is dead weight into anything airborne — Double-Edge is the closer.
