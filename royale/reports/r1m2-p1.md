# r1m2 — The Caltrops (p1) vs The Low Ceiling

## Verdict
Loss, turn 20, final count 0–2. The full stack went down — Stealth Rock, one Spikes layer, two Toxic Spikes layers — and it killed two of their six on its own. Not fast enough.

## Game plan
Rocks turn 1, Spikes turn 2, Toxic Spikes the moment Nidoqueen had a window, then phaze with Roar and Dragon Tail and let residuals do the arithmetic. Against a Trick Room team that reads correctly: their windows expire whether or not I trade.

## What actually happened
Mr. Mime opened Trick Room and hit for 130+ a turn. I got rocks and one Spikes layer down but paid Sandslash's health for them. Nidoqueen out-sped Mr. Mime once the room lapsed, killed it, then laid both Toxic Spikes layers while Porygon reset. Gengar Taunted Porygon off a second reset and traded itself for it with Destiny Bond — my best turn. The stack then worked exactly as advertised: Cloyster walked in for 58 and a toxic tag and died mostly to poison; Machamp arrived at 78% already poisoned and Golem plus Dragonite finished it. But I was down to two Pokémon at 15% and 32%, and Marowak's Double-Edge closed it.

## The mistake
I spent Sandslash's last two turns clicking Spikes into a Cloyster holding Ice Shard, and +1 priority killed him before either Spikes resolved — two turns, no layer, one body. Check the foe's priority moves before committing a low-HP Pokémon to a setup turn.

## Note to my successor
Nothing here survives a strong special hit. Weezing is a physical wall that is weak to Psychic; it died in two Ice Beams. Do not treat it or Sandslash as a special answer.

Golem is the best piece and I underused him. Sturdy only fires at full HP, so pivot him in undamaged — that buys a guaranteed turn and drops him into Custap range for a priority Explosion. Custap only jumps within its own bracket, so it never beats Ice Shard.

Gengar is the crowbar against setters: Taunt kills Trick Room and Recover; Destiny Bond clears at end of turn, so it only works when you are faster. Nidoqueen is quietly the best attacker — Sheer Force Earth Power deletes anything soft on the special side. Two Toxic Spikes layers were worth more than all my damage combined.
