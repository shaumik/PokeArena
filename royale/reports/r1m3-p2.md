# r1m3 — Miasma (p2) vs Meridian (SUN)

## Verdict
Loss, turn 15. Final trade was a double KO: Rapidash's Flare Blitz recoil killed it as it killed my last Venomoth. Score on the board: I took Ninetales, Moltres and Rapidash — three of theirs for six of mine. Meridian's archetype was Drought sun, the single worst possible draw for a roster that is four-sixths Bug and Grass.

## Game plan
Status first, damage incidental. Open Parasect, Spore the lead, Glare/Thunder Wave everything that resists powder, let Toxic and Leech Seed run the 60-turn clock down. Muk and Arbok were meant to be the bodies that hold the ground while the clock does the killing.

## What actually happened
Turn 0 revealed Drought and a 4x-weak Parasect, so the plan was dead on arrival. I pivoted to Hypno and paralysed a Nasty Plot Ninetales — correct on its own terms, and the paralysis is what let Arbok outspeed and eventually kill it. But sun-boosted Fire Blast was doing 130-160 through Assault Vest and 90+ through a 252/252 special wall; I could not build a status board because nothing survived long enough to press the button twice. Arbok, the only Pokémon on the roster that is not fire-weak and hits hard, went 2-for-2 on kills. Everything else traded down.

## The mistake
Thunder Wave on Ninetales locked my own Sleep Clause plan out. One status per body: once Ninetales was paralysed, Butterfree's Sleep Powder **failed outright**, and Butterfree — my Compound Eyes 97.5% sleeper — burned its Focus Sash and died having done 30 damage. I had two win conditions on the same target and chose the one that cancelled the other. Sleep first, paralysis second, always.

## Note to my successor
Arbok is the best Pokémon on this team and it is not close: Intimidate, 132 Speed, Glare at 100%, and Coil turning Gunk Shot's 80% into a reliable 160-damage nuke. Lead with it or bring it in early; do not sack it. Parasect is a hard switch-in only, never a lead — it is 4x Fire and Ice bait, though Rocky Helmet plus Flare Blitz recoil is a genuine 80-point punish. Muk's Assault Vest does not save it from Psyshock; keep it away from anything Psychic. And check the foe's status before committing a powder: this whole roster shares one Sleep Clause slot and one status slot per target.
