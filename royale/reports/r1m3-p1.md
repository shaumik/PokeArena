# r1m3 — Meridian (p1) vs Miasma

## Verdict
Win, turn 15. Miasma lost all six — Muk, Butterfree, Hypno, Parasect, Arbok, Venomoth. Meridian lost Ninetales, Moltres and Rapidash, the last to its own recoil on the winning hit.

## Game plan
Lead Ninetales, hang eight turns of noon with Drought plus Heat Rock, and spend every one of them killing. Nasty Plot early so each sun-boosted Fire Blast is a one-shot; the Fire back-line converts the window before it closes. Reading the engine first paid: Heat Rock really is eight turns, Chlorophyll really is x2 — but Harvest is registered inert, with no hooks at all. Exeggutor's Sitrus Berry is one-and-done, which quietly demoted it from creed piece to bench body. It never saw the field.

## What actually happened
The sun did its job. Fire Blast at noon took a third off Hypno turn one; the Nasty Plot ate a Thunder Wave but bought a +2 that vaporised Muk and burned Butterfree through its Focus Sash. The paralysis was accidentally a gift — already being statused made Butterfree's Sleep Powder fail. Then Arbok came in, Coiled, and Gunk Shot removed Ninetales and, a turn later, a full-health Moltres in one blow. With the setter dead the sky was gone for good at turn 8, and the rest was a brawl. Rapidash won it: Charcoal Flare Blitz through Hypno, through Parasect's Rocky Helmet, High Horsepower for Arbok's last sliver, and a final 2x Flare Blitz that traded itself for Venomoth.

## The mistake
Nasty Plot on turn 2 instead of a second Fire Blast. It looked free against a wall and cost me the paralysis that halved Ninetales' Speed for the rest of its life — which is exactly why Arbok, a base-80 snake I should have outrun, got the first move and the kill. Against a status team you do not stand still in front of your own engine for a boost you did not need.

## Note to my successor
Ninetales is not a sweeper, it is a lighter — the eight turns die with it, and Gunk Shot-tier physical attacks two-shot its 149 HP. Do not count on Harvest; Exeggutor is just a bulky Psychic here. Rapidash is the real MVP: 172 Speed, Charcoal, and coverage (High Horsepower for poison) that needs no sun at all. Flareon is Choice-locked and too slow to lead a comeback. Ration Fire Blast — 5 PP at 85% is two misses from a lost match.
