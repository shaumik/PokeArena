# Perish Row — r1m1

## Verdict
Loss. Guillotine Club (GLASS CANNON) took it 4-0 on the board at turn 22 of 60. I killed Pinsir (Rocky Helmet recoil) and Raichu (Toxic, one tick too late), and left Scyther and Hitmonlee badly poisoned and nearly dead on their bench. Perish Song was never sung.

## Game plan
Refuse to die, put a clock on everything, ride the turn-60 judges' decision on Pokémon standing. Toxic, Leech Seed and Rocky Helmet do the killing; Protect, Roost, Regenerator and Rest pay for the turns.

## What actually happened
Their damage broke the arithmetic the plan depends on. Technician Aerial Ace Scyther hit Snorlax — 267 HP — for 164, and Wigglytuff for 209. Nothing here resists Flying, so every switch-in paid a full hit and then died before it could act, because their whole team outsped mine. That is the structural loss: a staller that never gets a turn is just a queue of corpses. The residuals did work — Pinsir and Raichu died to them — but a Nasty Plot Raichu that OHKO'd Articuno for exactly 197 and Tangela for exactly 172 was faster than my clock.

## The mistake
Turn 17. Articuno was at full against a +2 Raichu and I clicked Freeze-Dry — 74 damage onto a target already dying to Toxic — instead of switching to Tangela, the only body on this roster that resists Electric. I had already lit a five-tick fuse; I did not need to swing. That cost my best remaining wall for nothing. Against an already-poisoned threat, the stall answer is the switch, never the chip.

## Note to my successor
The roster's fatal hole: no Flying resist, no Rock resist, only Poliwrath even neutral to Fighting.

Lead Tangela. Helmet plus Regenerator is the most turn-efficient thing here, and losing that Helmet to Knock Off cost a Pokémon's worth of chip. Switch-ins eat a full hit and cannot act, so never bring something in *to* use a move — bring it in to absorb, and spend free post-faint entries on whoever actually needs to move. Wigglytuff's Protect after a Sitrus is the cheapest turn on the board. Snorlax under 60% is a corpse against physical attackers: its Defense is 85, not its HP. Land Toxic on the first thing that sits still — it won two of my three kills. Perish Song is dead weight on a Lapras that never survives to click it.
