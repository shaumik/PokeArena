# Guillotine Club — Round 1, Match 1 (p1)

## Verdict
**Win, 4 standing to 0.** Lost Pinsir and Raichu. Scyther (20%), Dodrio (untouched), Persian (53%) and Hitmonlee (7%) survived. Ended turn 22 of 60.

## Game plan
Outspeed, click the super-effective button, never trade turns. Perish Row was a Toxic/Protect/Regenerator stall row. Chip damage is worthless against that — Regenerator and Roost refund it — so the creed sharpened into one-shots only: find the move that removes a body in a single turn, and refuse to grind.

## What actually happened
Pinsir burned three turns while they double-switched Lapras to Poliwrath to Tangela, dying to its own Close Combat drops, Life Orb recoil and a Rocky Helmet. Scyther did the heavy lifting — banded Technician Aerial Ace deleted Snorlax and Wigglytuff straight through a Protect stall. Hitmonlee whiffed Stone Edge twice on Articuno, so I spent it on Knock Off to strip Tangela's Rocky Helmet, unlocking every contact move I own. Persian U-turned the pivot war back my way. Raichu closed it: Nasty Plot on their Roost turn, then +2 Thunderbolt through Articuno, Lapras and Poliwrath, and +2 Focus Blast into Tangela's base-40 Special Defense. It fainted to Toxic the same turn its last Thunderbolt won the match.

## The mistake
**Turn 2: I clicked Close Combat a third time with Pinsir at 7 HP.** They had double-switched on each of the two prior turns and I still assumed the target would stand still. Tangela came in and Rocky Helmet killed my best attacker for free. Switching a turn earlier denies them that kill. Against a team that pivots every turn, "it can't survive this" is a hope, not a read.

## Note to my successor
- **Raichu is the win condition, not filler.** Nasty Plot plus Focus Blast is your only answer to a special wall. Hold it until you know what their wall is.
- **Scyther is the workhorse.** Banded Aerial Ace never misses and one-shots most neutral targets. Ice is 2x on it, not 4x.
- **Pinsir is a trap versus pivot teams.** Close Combat's drops plus Life Orb give it two turns total. Swords Dance is rarely worth the turn.
- **Hitmonlee's Stone Edge will miss.** Treat it as a Knock Off delivery system; its Sash makes any switch-in free.
- **Persian's U-turn is your only clean pivot.** Place Scyther or Raichu behind a resisted move rather than switching raw.
- Toxic beats this roster harder than any attack does. Once poisoned, stop planning three turns ahead.
