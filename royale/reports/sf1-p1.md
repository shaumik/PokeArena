## Verdict

Loss. The Low Ceiling 1, Meridian 0 — Cloyster left standing on turn 15. I took four of their six (Mr. Mime, Marowak, Machamp, Omastar) and lost all six of mine. Their archetype was Trick Room: two setters, 0 Speed IVs on everything, and four bodies that hit like a dropped safe.

## Game plan

Lead Ninetales, take the eight turns of noon, and kill inside them. Against a Trick Room team that plan inverts: my whole roster is built on Speed, and Speed is exactly what Trick Room confiscates. So the real plan became *deny the room* — kill both setters, and survive the windows I couldn't stop. I did read one thing correctly ahead of time: weather is a field condition with its own counter, so the sun does **not** die with Ninetales. Ninetales relit a second full eight turns on turn 10. Tell your successor that; the dossier I inherited said the opposite.

## What actually happened

Fire Blast broke Mr. Mime in two hits but not before the first room went up. Exeggutor traded itself into Marowak; Moltres finished it with an instant Solar Beam. Rapidash's Charcoal Flare Blitz put Machamp into its own Flame Orb burn. Then Cloyster — Skill Link, Icicle Spear, Liquidation, 180 Defense — deleted Vileplume and Flareon on consecutive turns, both before they moved. Ninetales relit the sun and one-shot Omastar through a 4x Solar Beam, killed Porygon, and died at 8 HP.

## The mistake

Turn 12. Moltres was at 41 HP and I switched it in front of Porygon to Roost, on the read that Porygon "had never attacked in two turns." Tri Attack did exactly 41. That was a coin-flip on an unrevealed moveset with my second-to-last Pokémon as the stake, and the alternative — Ninetales killing Porygon immediately — kept two bodies on the field. I traded a certainty for a hope and got neither.

## Note to my successor

Sun is re-lightable; hoard Ninetales, don't spend it early. Fire Blast's 5 PP is the real clock, not the weather. And against Trick Room, paralysis is not a disaster — it dropped Ninetales *under* Cloyster and won me the turn order. What beat me was Ice Shard: priority ignores the room entirely. Check for it before you bet your last Pokémon on speed tiers.
